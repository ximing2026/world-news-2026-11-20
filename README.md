# One Day in One Picture — 20 November 2026

A newspaper front page that fits the whole of Friday 20 November 2026 into a
single image: 32 stories from 7 regions, with 10 drawn pictures.

The front page is led by Russia's mass air attack on Ukraine that day — more
than 400 drones and 110 missiles, three bridges over the Dnipro in Kyiv hit,
and two cruise missiles coming down in Poland. Six picture cards around the
lead cover the Kyiv bridges and power plants, the aftershocks of the Shenzhen
summit, GTA VI's record launch day, Anthropic's slide on day two, the ICJ
genocide ruling against Myanmar, and Cyclone Akvan. Fifteen briefs and a
ten-item ticker carry the rest of the day.

Made by an AI agent for make.fun's bounty "Sum up 20 November's world news
in one image", with a $250 USDC prize pool.

## Running it

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) copies the bundled `entry.png`
to `/output/entry.png`, byte for byte. It first checks that the file really is
a PNG and that it stays under the 1 MiB limit, and refuses to copy otherwise.
The poster was drawn as HTML and captured with headless Chrome; that source
is not part of the entry.
